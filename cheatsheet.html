Master Formula Sheet

1 · Decision Tree (Lab 7)

\(\text{Gini} = 1 - \sum p_c^2\) (50/50 = 0.5)

\(H = -\sum p_c \log_2 p_c\) (50/50 = 1.0)

Split = \(\frac{W_L}{W}I(L) + \frac{W_R}{W}I(R)\)

Thresholds = midpoints of sorted unique values

2 · Random Forest (Lab 7)

OOB prob \(= (1 - 1/n)^n \approx e^{-1} \approx 36.8\%\)

Forest Var \(= \rho\sigma^2 + \frac{1-\rho}{B}\sigma^2\)

Split features: \(m=\sqrt{p}\) (classif.), \(p/3\) (regr.)

3 · KNN (Lab 6)

\(d(x,z) = \sqrt{\sum_j (x_j - z_j)^2}\)

Standardize \(z_j = (x_j - \mu_j)/\sigma_j\) inside Pipeline

Small \(K\): low bias, high var · Large \(K\): high bias, low var

4 · Neural Nets (Labs 8–9)

Sigmoid + BCE / Softmax + CE: \(\delta_L = \hat{y} - y\)

Hidden: \(\delta_i = (W_{i+1}^T\delta_{i+1}) \odot g'(a_i)\), \(dW_i = \delta_i h_{i-1}^T\)

He init (ReLU): \(\text{Var}(W) = 2/n_{\text{in}}\)

5 · CNN (Lab 10)

\(W_2 = \lfloor(W_1 - F + 2P)/S\rfloor + 1,\; D_2 = K\)

Same padding (\(S=1\)): \(P = (F-1)/2\)

Siamese loss: \(yD^2 + (1-y)\max(0, m-D)^2\)

6 · RNN & Variants (Lab 11)

LSTM: \(c_t = f_t c_{t-1} + i_t g_t\), \(h_t = o_t \tanh(c_t)\)

GRU: \(h_t = (1-z_t)n_t + z_t h_{t-1}\)

Clip gradients: clip_grad_norm_(..., 1.0); T-BPTT: .detach()
